PAIR publishes contributions by other researchers once a first set of the editor’s own contributions is online. *[Opening date to be set by Kevin.]*

## What PAIR publishes

Briefs, Case Profiles and Comparative Notes on AI used by parliaments, their administrations and parliamentary services. The scope and the reporting standards are set out in the [Method](/method/). Contributions outside that scope are not considered.

## Who can contribute

Researchers working on parliaments, public administration, AI or related fields.

*[Kevin to decide: whether staff of parliamentary administrations may write about their own institution, and on what terms.]*

## How it works

1. **Proposal.** A short email with the topic, the intended format, the main sources and any relationship to the institution or supplier concerned.
2. **Decision.** The editor decides whether the proposal fits PAIR and replies within *[period to be set]*.
3. **Draft.** The draft follows the Method: dated events, a deployment status for every system, linked sources with their source type, and interpretation labelled as such.
4. **Editorial review.** The editor checks sources, dates, status labels and clarity, and may ask for changes. This is editorial review, not peer review. *[Kevin to decide: whether Comparative Notes get a second reader.]*
5. **Approval.** The author approves the final version before publication.
6. **Publication.** The contribution appears with its received and accepted dates, the review note, the author’s statement of competing interests and the AI-use statement.

## Requirements

- Every substantive claim is linked to a source, and every source is given a source type.
- A statement of competing interests, including research access to or collaboration with the institutions discussed. “None” must be stated explicitly.
- An AI-use statement naming the tools and the tasks they assisted with.
- Interviews only where the consent obtained covers publication. No confidential research material.

## Rights and licence

Authors keep their copyright and publish their contribution under the [CC BY 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They confirm that they hold the rights to any material they include. PAIR may archive contributions with a DOI on Zenodo.

*[Kevin to confirm: PAIR charges no fees and pays no fees.]*

## Corrections

Corrections follow the same policy as the editor’s own contributions: they are dated and explained on the contribution. Authors are informed before a correction is published.
